Meet El Mustapha, Maître Artisan

“Clay is not merely Moroccan earth; it is an ancient dialogue between patience, water, fire, and human soul.”

Born and trained in the storied ceramic traditions of the Souss-Massa region, El Mustapha brings over three decades of ancestral knowledge to the heart of Agadir. Every vessel, tagine, and sculptural vase in our showroom is thrown by hand on traditional kick-wheels.

At ZWEN POTERIE, we celebrate raw terracotta textures, organic mineral glazes, and clean futuristic silhouettes that elevate Moroccan artisanal art into contemporary luxury architecture.

100% Natural Clay Locally harvested from Atlas mountain soils without artificial binders.
Ancestral Glazes Formulated with mineral oxides, copper, cobalt, and natural wood ash.
Intimate Sessions Private and small-group pottery masterclasses tailored to all skill levels.
Agadir Sanctuary A serene creative atelier just minutes from the Atlantic coast.
