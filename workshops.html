Important Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before attending your session at our Agadir studio.

Do I need prior pottery experience?

None whatsoever! Over 80% of our workshop guests have never touched a potter’s wheel before. Maître Artisan El Mustapha gently guides your hands through every step of centering, pulling, and shaping.

What should I wear to the workshop?

Wear comfortable clothing and short fingernails if possible. We provide traditional artisan aprons. Moroccan clay washes out completely from fabrics with warm water, but we advise leaving delicate jewelry at home.

When can I collect my finished pottery?

Raw clay vessels must dry slowly for 48–72 hours before their initial 980°C bisque firing. Pieces are ready for local pickup within 3 to 5 days. For travelers leaving Agadir sooner, we offer safe worldwide express postal shipping directly to your home.

Where is the atelier located in Agadir?

Our showroom and workshop are located in the peaceful artisan quarter of Agadir, just a quick 10-minute taxi ride from the beach promenade and marina. You can find our exact coordinates via our Google Maps link.
